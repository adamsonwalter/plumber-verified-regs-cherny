Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

VIC PlumberRegs ("we", "us") publishes a verified register of Victorian and Commonwealth plumbing and roofing regulations. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the app, why, and how you can access, correct, or ask us about it.

What we collect

Browsing and searching the register requires no information at all. If you create an account or subscribe, we hold:

  • Account details — your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored by us in plain text.
  • Saved regulations and jobs — the entries you save and any job names, notes, or groupings you create.
  • Billing information — if you subscribe, our payment processor (Stripe) shares your name, email, and limited payment method details (such as card type and last four digits) with us. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Support correspondence — anything you send us by email.

If you use the register without an account, your saved items stay on your own device and are never sent to us.

How we use it

To operate the register, authenticate you, sync your saves and jobs across devices, process subscription payments, and respond when you contact support. We do not sell or trade your personal information.

We will only email you about product updates or other things we make, beyond the operation of your account, if you separately opt in — and every such email carries a way to unsubscribe. Opting out of that does not affect emails about your account or payments, which we need to send regardless.

Where it's held

Your account and app data are held with Supabase, and payments are processed by Stripe. Both are reputable providers used widely by Australian businesses, and using them means some of your information may be processed on servers located outside Australia, including in the United States. We choose providers who maintain their own strong security and privacy commitments as a condition of using them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are never visible to us, and account data is access-controlled so you can only see your own. Card numbers never touch our servers — Stripe handles that directly.

Access and correction

Email privacy@plumberregs.co to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. We'll respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, email privacy@plumberregs.co first so we can put it right. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be flagged in the app.
